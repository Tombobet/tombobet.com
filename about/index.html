About

I’m Tom. Industrial designer who grew up in ecom.

I’m 22 and French, finishing an industrial design master’s at ISD Rubika. I speak French, English and Spanish.

For five years I’ve run two tracks side by side. School taught me how products are designed and made. Ecom, on my own time, taught me how they get sold. That mix is my edge.

How I got here

A few places that shaped me.

  1. At 15

    A car-drawing business

    It put me in rooms with entrepreneurs and lawyers. That’s where the drive to build started.

  2. At 18

    ISD Rubika

    Industrial design master’s in France. That’s where my life really started.

  3. At 19

    Montreal, Canada

    A semester of study. On the side, I resold clothes and bikes, and tried to launch a car events business.

  4. At 20

    Interior architecture internship, Cognac

    Luxury private houses, down to the detail.

  5. At 20

    Barcelona, Spain

    Transportation design at IED, and a network that keeps growing.

  6. At 21

    Milestone watches

    Co-founded my first ecom business. We tried to design and sell our own timepiece.

  7. At 21

    Internship at Crooked, Germany

    Five months at an ecom brand selling motorcycle parts and accessories. I saw how an online business runs from the inside.

  8. At 22

    Freelance, then a studio

    Design missions for clients: a book, a video shoot, a brand identity. Now I’m bringing it all together into an ecom design studio.
