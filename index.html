Three reasons your brand isn’t an asset yet.

  1. You sell a product you don’t own.

    The same item, from the same supplier, is in a hundred other stores. Anything anyone can source isn’t an asset.→ The fix is a product that’s yours alone (product design).

  2. Customers buy the deal, not the brand.

    Without an identity, nobody remembers you or comes back, and you can’t charge more. You’re one cheaper copy away from losing them.→ The fix is an identity people remember (brand).

  3. Your growth is rented.

    Every sale comes from paid attention. Stop the ads and the store stops. Nothing you make keeps working for you.→ The fix is creative that builds recognition with every ad (the moat).
